What does it take to find a tenant for your rental?

A walk through of the work, before the first rent check clears.

“The market is the final judge.”

“Paperwork that holds up.”

  1. 01

    Pricing analysis

    Comps and recent days on market. A proposed price with the reasoning. The market is the final judge.

  2. 02

    Marketing

    Photos and listing copy. Listed on MLS first. Syndicates to Zillow, Apartments.com, Rent.com, Realtor.com and more.

  3. 03

    Fielding inquiries

    Most leads ghost or do not qualify. Triage on well defined criteria.

  4. 04

    Showings

    Open houses and private showings. Weekends and evenings. Whatever it takes.

  5. 05

    Tenant screening

    Protects the next twelve months, and beyond. Follows federal, state and local fair housing laws.

  6. 06

    Lease and move in

    Paperwork that holds up. Walk through that documents condition.
