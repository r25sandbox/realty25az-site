# Five filters for choosing a housing market.

Five questions we use when an owner of a single-family rental in North Scottsdale and nearby asks whether a housing market still makes sense.

The house is local. The owner may live somewhere else. A headline price is not the whole answer. These filters are how we look at the market around a single-family rental.

## Five filters.

### 1. Buyable housing

Can we acquire desirable single-family homes at sensible prices?

### 2. Durable cash flow

Do rents cover property taxes, insurance, maintenance, and the other costs, and still leave a return the owner can accept?

### 3. Friendly jurisdiction

Are property rights, regulations, and the setting for independent enterprise favorable and predictable?

### 4. Credible economic base

Are there durable employers, diverse industries, and room for independent producers whose work can travel with them?

### 5. Livability, especially climate

Is this a place people choose to live for a season or for the year when their income does not have to be earned in that city?

## The shift

The Industrial Age brought workers to production. Renaissance 2.0 may let productive capacity follow people, so housing demand can follow livability and the choice of jurisdiction more closely.
