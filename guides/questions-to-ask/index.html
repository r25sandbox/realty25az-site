Owner Guides

Questions to ask a property manager before you sign.

Twelve questions. One line on why each one matters. Ask them of any manager, including us, before you sign in North Scottsdale and nearby.

“The monthly fee is one number. Ask what sits on the other lines.”

Ask these before you sign.

  1. 1

    What is the full fee, including markups?

    The monthly fee is one number. A markup on repairs can cost more than that fee over a year.

  2. 2

    What do you charge when the home is vacant?

    The month the rent stops is the month the fee should be easiest to explain.

  3. 3

    What do you charge to find a tenant?

    This is often the largest one-time bill. Ask how it is calculated before a tenant is in the home.

  4. 4

    Who do I talk to when something breaks?

    You want direct access to the Principal, not a call queue.

  5. 5

    Do you hold a reserve, and how much?

    A reserve is your money. Know the amount, what it pays for, and when it comes back.

  6. 6

    Which repairs can start without my approval?

    A dollar cap in writing tells you which jobs wait for you and which ones can start.

  7. 7

    What is in the owner report, and when does it arrive?

    The report is how you see rent, expenses, and the balance without chasing anyone.

  8. 8

    What does the lease lock in at move in?

    The lease is the paperwork you will rely on if there is a dispute later.

  9. 9

    How do you screen, and which fair housing laws do you follow?

    Screening protects the next twelve months, and beyond. It has to follow federal, state, and local fair housing laws.

  10. 10

    How do I end the agreement?

    Ask about notice, any fee to leave, and what happens to the records, the deposit, and the reserve.

  11. 11

    Who answers after hours?

    Ask who is on call, and which repairs can wait until the next business day.

  12. 12

    How do you document the home before the tenant moves in?

    A walkthrough that records condition is what you compare against at move out.
