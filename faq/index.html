Why do you charge $125 a month when I have seen lower fees elsewhere?

A lower headline fee is a fair question. The difference is what sits behind the number. Some companies route repairs through a contractor they own, so every repair bill carries a markup you never see on the fee line. One marked-up job can erase years of that fee difference.

Our model: $125 flat. Monthly management is $0 when vacant, and there is $0 markup on repairs. Our vetted contractors bill you directly. Nothing else.

The fee is one number. The question is what is behind the other numbers.
