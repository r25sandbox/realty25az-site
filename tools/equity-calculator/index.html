The largest cash out is the refi that keeps monthly cash flow at or above $0. The loan cannot exceed 80% of the property value. This tool does not apply a 5% appreciation rate. The term is 30 years (360 months). Rent is counted in full, with no vacancy allowance. At 0% interest, a fixed loan spreads the principal over those months, and an interest-only loan has no monthly payment. Cash flow is rent minus the monthly expenses, including the new mortgage.
